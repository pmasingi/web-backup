Study tips, techniques, and resources to enhance your academic journey

Unlock Your Learning Potential

Discover proven study techniques, time management strategies, and resources that will transform your approach to learning and help you achieve academic success.

Proven Study Techniques

Active Recall

Test yourself regularly instead of just re-reading notes. This strengthens memory and identifies knowledge gaps.

How to implement:

  • Create flashcards for key concepts
  • Practice explaining topics out loud
  • Take practice tests regularly
  • Teach concepts to someone else

Spaced Repetition

Review material at increasing intervals to move information from short-term to long-term memory.

How to implement:

  • Review new material after 1 day
  • Review again after 3 days
  • Review after 1 week
  • Review after 2 weeks, then monthly

Pomodoro Technique

Break study sessions into focused 25-minute intervals with short breaks to maintain concentration.

How to implement:

  • Study for 25 minutes focused
  • Take a 5-minute break
  • Repeat 3-4 times
  • Take a longer 15-30 minute break

Mind Mapping

Create visual representations of information to help understand relationships between concepts.

How to implement:

  • Start with main topic in center
  • Branch out to subtopics
  • Use colors and images
  • Connect related concepts

ADHD-Friendly Study Tips

Students with ADHD often face unique challenges in traditional learning environments. Here are strategies that can help:

Use Background Music

Instrumental music or white noise can help maintain focus and reduce distractions.

Break Tasks into Smaller Chunks

Large assignments can feel overwhelming. Break them into smaller, manageable tasks.

Incorporate Movement

Take movement breaks, use a standing desk, or try fidget tools while studying.

Use Visual Aids

Colorful notes, diagrams, and visual organizers can help maintain attention and improve understanding.

Minimize Distractions

Create a dedicated study space and use apps to block distracting websites during study time.

Time Management & Productivity

Create a Study Schedule

Plan your study sessions in advance and stick to a consistent routine.

  • Use a planner or digital calendar
  • Block out specific times for each subject
  • Include breaks and leisure time
  • Review and adjust weekly

Prioritize Tasks

Focus on the most important and urgent tasks first.

  • Use the Eisenhower Matrix
  • Tackle difficult subjects when you're most alert
  • Complete assignments well before deadlines
  • Break large projects into smaller milestones

Avoid Procrastination

Develop strategies to overcome the urge to delay important tasks.

  • Start with the easiest part of a task
  • Use the "2-minute rule" for quick tasks
  • Remove distractions from your environment
  • Reward yourself for completing tasks

Maintain Work-Life Balance

Ensure you have time for rest, hobbies, and social activities.

  • Set boundaries for study time
  • Schedule regular breaks and leisure activities
  • Get adequate sleep (7-9 hours)
  • Exercise regularly to reduce stress

Effective Note-Taking Methods

Cornell Method

Divide your page into three sections: notes, cues, and summary.

Best for: Lectures, textbook reading, organized review

Mind Mapping

Create visual diagrams showing relationships between concepts.

Best for: Visual learners, brainstorming, complex topics

Outline Method

Use bullet points and indentation to show hierarchy of information.

Best for: Structured content, logical sequences

Charting Method

Organize information in tables and columns for easy comparison.

Best for: Comparing information, data-heavy subjects
