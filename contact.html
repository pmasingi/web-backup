Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can I start tutoring sessions?

We can usually arrange your first session within 2-3 days of initial contact. For urgent needs, we often accommodate same-day or next-day sessions.

Do you offer both online and in-person sessions?

Yes! We offer flexible options including online sessions via video call and in-person sessions in the Johannesburg area. You can mix both formats based on your needs.

What are your rates?

Our standard rate is R150/hour for online and nearby in-person sessions, and R200/hour for sessions requiring travel. We also offer discounted package deals for multiple sessions.

How do I request pre-recorded videos?

Simply contact us with the specific topic or concept you need help with. Videos are R80 for high school content and R100 for NATED/college level content.

What if I'm not satisfied with a session?

Your satisfaction is our priority. If you're not completely satisfied with a session, we'll work with you to address any concerns and ensure you get the support you need.

Do you provide study materials?

Yes, we provide customized study materials, practice problems, and resources tailored to your specific needs and curriculum requirements.
