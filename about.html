About Math & Matter

Where your learning matters - transforming academic challenges into confident achievements

Our Story

Math & Matter was founded with a simple yet powerful belief: every student has the potential to excel academically when given the right support, guidance, and learning environment.

We understand that traditional classroom settings don't always cater to individual learning styles and paces. That's why we've created a personalized tutoring experience that adapts to each student's unique needs, helping them build confidence and achieve their academic goals.

Our approach goes beyond just improving grades. We focus on developing critical thinking skills, problem-solving abilities, and a genuine love for learning that will serve our students throughout their academic journey and beyond.

Meet the Founder

Charity Masingi

Founder of Math and Matter

Hi! My name is Charity Masingi, the founder of Math and Matter. I am currently an Applied Maths and Computer Science student, with a deep passion for science, technology, and making education more accessible.

Growing up, I was a high-performing student and achieved five distinctions in my matric year, including Mathematics, Physical Sciences, and Life Sciences. But when I entered university, I faced unexpected challenges. I was later diagnosed with ADHD, and suddenly many of my struggles made sense.

If I had known about my ADHD earlier, my academic journey would have looked very different. Unfortunately, I often felt that tutors and support systems did not understand how to teach students with unique learning needs or different ways of processing information.

That's why I created Math and Matter: to build a tutoring environment where no student feels left behind. My mission is to make learning clear, accessible, and tailored, whether you're preparing for exams, catching up on missed concepts, or aiming for top results.

Here, we combine personalized teaching methods, real study strategies, and subject mastery to help students unlock their full potential.
